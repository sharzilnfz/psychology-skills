# Module 40: Coping Toolkit (Preparation)

**Goal:** Build a concrete change plan with coping strategies and support.

## Change Plan Elements

1. **Goal** — Specific, measurable, realistic. Not necessarily abstinence.
   - "Reduce from 5 drinks/night to 2"
   - "No gaming before 8pm on weekdays"
   - "One day per week without [substance]"

2. **Start Date** — Specific, not "soon."

3. **Coping Toolkit:**
   - **Urge surfing:** Notice the craving, don't fight it. It peaks in ~20 min.
   - **HALT check:** Hungry, Angry/Anxious, Lonely, Tired?
   - **Delay and distract:** 15-minute rule — wait 15 minutes before acting.
   - **Play the tape forward:** "If I use, what happens in 1 hour? Tomorrow?"
   - **DBT distress tolerance:** TIPP, ACCEPTS (see `psych-session-protocol/references/therapeutic-tools.md`)
   - **CFT compassionate self:** "What would my compassionate self do right now?"
   - **Support activation:** Who to call, what to say

4. **Support Network:**
   - "Who knows about your plan? Who can support you?"
   - "What role does each person play?"
   - "What professional support do you have access to?"

5. **Harm Reduction Backup:**
   - "If you can't stick to the plan, what is the safest alternative?"
   - "How can you use more safely if you do use?"

## Adapting to Processing Preferences

If the user has shared how they process (e.g., ADHD, sensory needs,
thinking style), adapt this module accordingly. Use their language, not
diagnostic labels. Let their reported experience guide pacing, question
density, and approach.

## Saturation

Concrete goal + start date + 3+ coping strategies + support network + harm
reduction backup. Write commitments to `state.json.commitmentLog`.
