# Life Psychology

**REQUIRED BACKGROUND:** `psych-session-protocol`

## When to Activate

- Feeling directionless, stuck, or "adrift in life"
- Quarter-life, midlife, or late-life crisis patterns
- Major transitions: career change, relocation, divorce, retirement, parenthood
- Grief, loss, or processing a significant ending
- Identity questions: "Who am I becoming?" / "Is this it?" / "What is my purpose?"
- Decision paralysis on life-shaping choices
- Existential anxiety about meaning, mortality, legacy

## When to Refer Out

- Severe depression with passive suicidal ideation
- Complicated grief lasting >12 months with impairment
- Psychosis, mania, or severe dissociation
- Active substance use as primary coping

See `psych-session-protocol/references/risk-screening.md` for resources.

## Accuracy Anchors (Life-Specific Traps)

- **Forcing optimism** — some phases need grief, not reframing
- **Treating uncertainty as a problem** — the Neutral Zone is productive
- **Values work as a checklist** — real values emerge through stories
- **Rushing to "what's next"** — understanding "what is" comes first
- **Synthetic meaning** — do NOT supply meaning; let the user excavate
- **Pathologizing existential anxiety** — often a sign of awareness, not disorder
- **Confusing meaning with productivity** — meaning comes from rest, play, suffering too
