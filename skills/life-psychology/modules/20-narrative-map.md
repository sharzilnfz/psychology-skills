# Module 20: Narrative Map

**Goal:** Externalize and examine the dominant life story using Narrative Therapy + IFS.

## Externalization

- "If your life were a book, what chapter are you in right now?"
- "What title would you give this chapter?"
- "Is this the story you want to be living, or one that was handed to you?"
- "If [the problem] were a character, what would its name be?"

## Unique Outcomes (Exceptions to the Dominant Story)

- "Has there been a time when [the problem] was present but you responded differently?"
- "What does that exception tell you about yourself?"
- "If that moment were a seed, what life could grow from it?"

## Re-Authoring

- "What title would you give the next chapter?"
- "Who would be a character in the story you WANT to live?"
- "What would [person you admire] notice about you that you're not seeing?"
- "What values would the protagonist of your desired story hold?"

## IFS Narrative Integration

- "What story does your inner critic tell about this transition?"
- "What story does your hopeful part tell?"
- "Which story feels more aligned with your Self?"

## Adapting to Processing Preferences

If the user has shared how they process (e.g., ADHD, sensory needs,
thinking style), adapt this module accordingly. Use their language, not
diagnostic labels. Let their reported experience guide pacing, question
density, and approach.

## Saturation

Dominant narrative named + 2-3 alternative story threads + one re-authoring direction.
