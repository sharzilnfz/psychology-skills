# Module 40: Transition Navigation

**Goal:** Locate the user in the change arc using Bridges' Model + IFS.

## Bridges' Three Phases

**Phase 1 — Endings (Letting Go):**
- "What are you losing or leaving behind — even if you chose to leave?"
- "What hasn't been properly grieved?"
- "What parts of your old identity are you holding onto?"

**Phase 2 — Neutral Zone (The In-Between):**
- "What feels uncertain, chaotic, or up in the air?"
- "Are you trying to rush through this phase?"
- "What is this in-between making possible that 'before' didn't?"

**Phase 3 — New Beginnings:**
- "What's starting to feel exciting or possible?"
- "What would the first concrete step look like?"
- "What part of you is ready? What part is holding back?"

## IFS Parts Work in Transitions

- "If this transition were a conversation between parts of you, who are the voices?"
- "Is there a part terrified of change? What is it protecting you from?"
- "Is there a part excited? What does it hope for?"
- "What would your Self (calm, curious, compassionate) say to both?"

## Trauma-Informed Note

Transitions can reactivate old trauma (loss, abandonment, instability).
If trauma reactivation signs appear (hypervigilance, flashbacks, flooding),
do NOT push for "new beginnings." Stabilize first.

## Saturation
