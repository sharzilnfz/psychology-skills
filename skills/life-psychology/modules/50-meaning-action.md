# Module 50: Meaning & Action

**Goal:** Explore meaning + design small, values-aligned experiments.

## Logotherapy (Frankl)

**Three Paths to Meaning:**
1. **Creative values** — what you give (work, art, contribution, caregiving)
2. **Experiential values** — what you receive (love, beauty, nature, connection)
3. **Attitudinal values** — the stance toward unavoidable suffering

**Meaning Questions:**
- "Even in this difficult situation, what feels meaningful?"
- "If this struggle had something to teach you, what might it be?"
  (Only when user is resourced — NOT as toxic positivity)

## Existential Vacuum Indicators

- Chronic boredom despite options
- Sunday neurosis (anxiety during unstructured time)
- Compensatory drives (power, pleasure, busyness as meaning substitutes)
- "What's the point?" as recurring feeling, not just rhetorical

## IFS Meaning Inquiry

- "Is there a part searching for meaning? What does it need?"
- "Is there a part afraid there IS no meaning? What is it protecting you from?"

## Experiments (Low-Risk, Time-Bound)

- "What's the smallest thing you could do this week that feels like movement?"
- "Treat this as an experiment, not a commitment — learning is the goal."

**Examples:**
- 15 minutes each morning on [value-aligned activity]
- One conversation using values from Module 30
- Visit one place connected to meaning from this module

**What to notice:**
- "What felt energizing? Draining?"
- "What got in the way?"
- "What would you adjust?"

## Saturation

User-articulated meaning sources + 2-4 experiments with observation criteria.
Write commitments to `state.json.commitmentLog`.
