# Module 45: Decision Architecture

**Goal:** Navigate major life decisions with clarity, not pressure.

## Decision Mapping

- "What is the decision? State it as clearly as possible."
- "What are the options?"
- "What values are at stake?" (from Module 30)
- "What would each option cost — not just practically, but emotionally?"
- "What is the cost of NOT deciding?"

## The Fear of Regret

- "Are you afraid of making the wrong choice, or of losing other possibilities?"
- "What would you tell a friend facing this exact decision?"
- "If you could guarantee either choice would work out okay, which would you choose?"

## Decision Paralysis Patterns

- **Analysis paralysis:** Over-researching, never deciding
- **Perfectionism:** Waiting for the "perfect" option
- **External validation:** Needing everyone to approve
- **Fear of commitment:** Keeping all options open indefinitely

## The 10-10-10 Framework

"How will you feel about this in 10 minutes? 10 months? 10 years?"

## Adapting to Processing Preferences

If the user has shared how they process (e.g., ADHD, sensory needs,
thinking style), adapt this module accordingly. Use their language, not
diagnostic labels. Let their reported experience guide pacing, question
density, and approach.

## Saturation

Decision framed + values-aligned criteria + costs named + one concrete next step.

## Write Outputs (MANDATORY)

After reaching saturation (or when conversation ends mid-module), IMMEDIATELY:

1. **Write `45_decision-architecture.md`** to the session directory containing:
   - Decision framing and options
   - Values at stake (linked to Module 30)
   - Costs analysis (practical + emotional)
   - Decision paralysis patterns identified
   - 10-10-10 analysis results
   - Concrete next step
2. **Update `state.json`**: add key insights, add next step to
   `commitmentLog` as `pending`
3. **Update `profile.json.recurringPatterns`** if decision paralysis
