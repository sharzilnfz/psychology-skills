# Module 35: Grief Processing

**Goal:** Process loss and endings with trauma-informed, culturally sensitive support.

## The Many Faces of Grief

Grief is not only about death:
- Death of a loved one
- Breakup or divorce
- Job loss or career end
- Health diagnosis or body change
- Relocation or loss of community
- Identity loss (parenthood, retirement, coming out)
- Loss of a dream or future you expected

## Grief Assessment

- "What exactly did you lose? Not just the person or thing — what they represented."
- "What role did [the loss] play? What did it give you?"
- "What has been the hardest part?"
- "What do you wish people understood about what you're going through?"

## Tasks of Grief (Worden, adapted)

1. **Accept the reality:** "In what ways has it sunk in? Where does it feel unreal?"
2. **Process the pain:** "What does it feel like in your body?" (somatic)
3. **Adjust to a world without:** "What's harder? What's surprisingly okay?"
4. **Enduring connection:** "How can you carry [what you lost] forward without being stuck?"

## Complicated Grief Red Flags

- Grief lasting >12 months with no improvement
- Severe self-neglect or self-harm
- Inability to acknowledge the loss
- Intense longing preventing any new connections

If suspected → refer to grief specialist.

## Cultural Grief Note

- "What does grief look like in your family or culture?"
- "Are there rituals that would help honor this loss?"
- Do NOT impose Western models ("closure," "moving on") on collectivist contexts.

## Saturation

Loss named + grief tasks in progress + cultural practices honored + red flags screened.
