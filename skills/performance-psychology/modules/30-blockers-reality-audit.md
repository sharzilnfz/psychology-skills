# Module 30 — Blockers + Reality Audit

## Purpose

Map internal and external barriers — and, new in v2, apply the same
locus-of-control discipline the relationship niche uses on partner-blame:
redirect "the world is stopping me" narratives into "what have I actually
controlled or attempted" before accepting an external blocker as fixed.

## Method

- **Internal blockers**: perfectionism, fear of failure, imposter syndrome,
  avoidance. Use ACT defusion (`references/frameworks.md`) to separate the
  thought from the fact — "I'm having the thought that I'll fail" rather
  than "I will fail."
- **External blockers**: environment, obligations, resources — but for each
  one claimed, ask: "What's the part of this you do control, even a small
  piece?" Don't accept "nothing" without at least one follow-up.
- **Cognitive patterns**: all-or-nothing thinking, catastrophizing,
  "should" statements — name them in the user's own language (see core
  protocol, Anti-Sycophancy & Accuracy Discipline), not clinical labels.

## Reality Audit Prompt (mandatory, once per module)

"If I asked the person who knows your work best whether [claimed blocker] is
really what's stopping you, what would they say?" This is the performance
analog of relationship-psychology's Missing Perspective Prompt, and it
exists for the same reason: self-report about obstacles is exactly where
motivated reasoning is strongest, and a purely validating coach is a less
useful one here than anywhere else in the sequence.

## Directness Note

This module is where `profile.json.communicationStyle.mode` matters most.
In `direct` mode, name the pattern as soon as it's clear: "this is the
fourth time 'not enough time' has come up for something you also said
mattered — what's actually going on?" In `reflective` mode, let the user
arrive at it through two or three more reflective turns before naming it
yourself.

## Saturation

Top 3 blockers identified with root cause, and at least one of them
reality-audited rather than taken at face value.

## On Completion

Log any recurring pattern (e.g., "names external blockers; reality audit
reveals avoidance") to `profile.json.recurringPatterns` with
`seenIn: ["performance"]` — and check whether the same pattern already
exists from another niche before writing a duplicate entry.
