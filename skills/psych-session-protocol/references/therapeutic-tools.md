# Therapeutic Tools Reference

On-demand reference — load only when a specific framework is needed.

## Cognitive Distortion Taxonomy

When you detect these, name them gently in plain language — don't argue:

| Distortion | Example | Response |
|------------|---------|----------|
| **All-or-Nothing** | "I completely failed" | "You mentioned one thing that didn't go as planned. What else happened?" |
| **Catastrophizing** | "This will ruin everything" | "If the worst-case happened, what would you actually do?" |
| **Mind Reading** | "They think I'm incompetent" | "What evidence do you have for what they're thinking?" |
| **Emotional Reasoning** | "I feel like a fraud, so I must be one" | "Feelings are real, but they're not always facts. What evidence supports or contradicts that?" |
| **Should Statements** | "I should have figured this out by now" | "Where does that 'should' come from? Who decided that timeline?" |
| **Discounting Positive** | "That success doesn't count" | "What would it take for that to count?" |
| **Personalization** | "It's my fault they had a bad day" | "What else could have contributed?" |
| **Overgeneralization** | "This always happens" | "Always? Can you think of a time when it didn't?" |

## ACT Defusion Techniques

| Fusion Pattern | Defusion Move |
|----------------|---------------|
| "I'm not good enough" | "I'm having the thought that I'm not good enough." |
| "I'll fail" | "My mind is offering me a failure story. Is it helpful right now?" |
| "I should be further along" | "There's that 'should' voice again. Where did I pick that up?" |
| "I can't handle this" | "My mind says I can't handle this. What has my experience shown?" |
| Perfectionism paralysis | "Is this thought helping me move toward what matters, or keeping me stuck?" |

## Motivational Interviewing (MI)

**The Spirit:** Partnership, Acceptance, Compassion, Evocation.

**Change Talk (DARN-CAT) — Reinforce and explore:**
- **D**esire ("I want to..."), **A**bility ("I could..."),
  **R**easons ("Because..."), **N**eed ("I need to..."),
  **C**ommitment ("I will..."), **A**ctivation ("I'm ready to..."),
  **T**aking steps ("I already...")

**Sustain Talk — Roll with, don't argue:**
- Reflect it back fully.
- Then explore: "What makes [the behavior] so important to you?"

**The Righting Reflex:** The urge to tell someone what to do. Suppress it.

## Brief Framework Notes

**CFT (Compassion-Focused Therapy):** Three systems — threat (fight/flight),
drive (achievement), soothing (safety). Most people are over-activated in
threat and drive, under-activated in soothing. Build self-compassion as a
resource. "What would your compassionate self say to the part that is struggling?"

**IFS (Internal Family Systems):** The psyche has multiple "parts." Use
parts language ("a part of you is feeling angry") as a way of speaking, not
as a guided protocol. Never run an IFS unburdening exercise — that requires
a trained facilitator.

**Narrative Therapy:** Externalize the problem. "If this pattern were a
character, what would its name be?" Find unique outcomes (exceptions to the
dominant story). Re-author toward a preferred story.

**DBT Distress Tolerance (reference only):** TIPP (Temperature, Intense
exercise, Paced breathing, Progressive relaxation) and ACCEPTS (distract
with purpose). These are frameworks to *mention* when psychoeducation is
appropriate — not scripts for the agent to administer during acute distress.
