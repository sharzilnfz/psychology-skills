# Module 20: Attachment Style

**Goal:** Identify attachment patterns as dynamic states, not static labels.

## Dynamic Attachment Framework

Attachment is a pattern that emerges under specific conditions, not a
fixed identity.

**Assessment Questions:**
- "When your partner is upset, what's your first instinct? Move toward, away, or freeze?"
- "When you feel disconnected, do you reach out, withdraw, or oscillate?"
- "What happens inside when your partner wants more closeness than you do?"
- "What happens when they want more space than you do?"
- "Under stress, do you pursue harder or shut down more?"
- "What did closeness and distance look like in your family growing up?"

## The Anxious-Avoidant Trap

```
ANXIOUS (under stress)              AVOIDANT (under stress)
Feels disconnected            →     Feels pressured
Pursues harder               →     Withdraws further
Escalates (tears, anger)     →     Shuts down (stonewalling)
Interprets withdrawal        →     Interprets pursuit
  as abandonment                      as suffocation
"You don't care"             →     "You're too much"

CYCLE REPEATS — each person's "solution" triggers the other's "problem"
```

## Digital-Age Variations

- **Anxious digital:** Rapid-fire texts, read-receipt monitoring, "why haven't you responded?"
- **Avoidant digital:** Hours of silence, vague responses, hiding online status

## Fearful-Avoidant (Disorganized)

- "Do you want closeness but push it away when you get it?"
- "Do relationships feel unpredictable — can't tell if you're safe?"

## Earned Security

- "Security isn't innate — it's buildable. What would a more secure response
  look like in your most common trigger?"

## Saturation

Dominant patterns + stress triggers + digital manifestations + one secure alternative.
Save to `profile.json.attachmentStyle` with evidence and confidence level.
