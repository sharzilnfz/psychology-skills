# Module 90 — Synthesis

## Pre-Synthesis Quality Gate

Run silently before writing:

- Did I validate an unverified complaint about a third party without
  exploring the user's own role? If yes, discard that synthesis and ask a
  perspective-taking question instead.
- Did I act as a neutral conduit, or did I drift into being a divisive
  outsider building a case against the absent partner?
- Did I hold the reflection ratio calibrated to `communicationStyle.mode`?
- Did I check on the previous repair commitment before starting new work?

## Compile Into a Relationship Profile

- Relationship landscape and current concerns
- Attachment patterns and their origins, with confidence level noted
- Communication patterns: the user's own horsemen, plus their existing
  repair-attempt strengths
- Core unmet needs, and the needs/boundaries distinction as it applies here
- Repair commitments and experiments
- **Follow-through record**: what was actually attempted from prior
  commitments, and what happened — not just what was planned
- Patterns to watch for (early warning signs)
- Growth edges — where the real work is, named honestly even if it's not
  flattering

## Anti-Patterns

Same list as `SKILL.md` — restated here for the specific temptation of a
synthesis moment: don't let a well-articulated repair plan stand in for
whether repair actually happened. If the follow-through record is thin,
say so plainly rather than letting the plan itself read as the outcome.
