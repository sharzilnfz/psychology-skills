# Module 90: Synthesis

**Goal:** Compile a complete relationship profile.

## Pre-Synthesis Quality Gate

- Did I validate an unverified complaint about a partner without exploring the user's role?
- Did I maintain the Missing Perspective Prompt?
- Did I act as a communication conduit, not a divisive outsider?
- Did I ensure all session checkpoints were met?

## Compiled Profile

1. **Relationship Landscape** (stage, history, concerns, partner perspective)
2. **Attachment Patterns** (dynamic states, stress triggers)
3. **Communication Patterns** (horsemen, repair attempts, bid responses, conflict cycles)
4. **Core Unmet Needs** (top 3 + NVC expression + differentiation moves)
5. **Repair Commitments** (3-5 specific actions + re-engagement protocol)
6. **Maintenance Plan** (hygiene practices + booster triggers)
7. **Readiness Trajectory** (trend, victory log, patterns)
8. **Growth Edges** (where the real work continues)
9. **Situational Work** (if explored: desire/intimacy findings, digital agreements)

## After Synthesis

- Write durable data to `profile.json`
- Set `currentPhase` to `80-maintenance`
- Schedule first booster (default: 30 days)
