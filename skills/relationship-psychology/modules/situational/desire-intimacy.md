# Module 45: Desire & Intimacy

**Goal:** Explore erotic connection, desire, and intimacy in relationships.

## Perel's Erotic Intelligence Framework

**The Erotic vs. The Domestic:**
- Eroticism requires tension, mystery, transgression, imagination
- Domesticity requires safety, predictability, transparency, routine
- Long-term relationships must hold BOTH — complementary, not opposites

## Desire Questions

- "When do you feel most turned on by your partner? What were the conditions?"
- "What is the difference between sex you have and sex you WANT to have?"
- "Is there a part of yourself you don't bring into the relationship?"
- "What is your erotic blueprint — anticipation, sensuality, dominance,
  submission, novelty, emotional connection?"

## The Shadow of Desire

- "What do you fantasize about that you don't share?"
- "What would feel too vulnerable to ask for?"
- "What sexuality did you leave behind when you committed?"

## Cultivating Erotic Space

- **Novelty in context:** New places, roles, rituals
- **Mystery and privacy:** "You don't need to know everything about me"
- **Separate-together:** Individual interests create distance that fuels desire
- **The erotic mind:** What stories or scenarios activate imagination?

## Intimacy Beyond Sex

- "What makes you feel truly seen?"
- "When do you feel closest without being physical?"
- "What could they do that would make you feel deeply known?"
