# Module 48: Digital Boundaries

**Goal:** Design healthy digital relationship practices.

## Texting Patterns

- "What are your unwritten rules about texting? Response time expectations?"
- "Do you use texting for logistics, emotional connection, or both?"
- "Have you ever had a fight that started or escalated over text?"

## Social Media Boundaries

- "What do you share about the relationship online? What is private?"
- "How do you feel about your partner's social media use?"
- "Has social media triggered jealousy or comparison?"

## Dating Apps (if relevant)

- "How do you manage the paradox of choice?"
- "Boundaries around exclusivity and app usage?"
- "Transition from app to real-world connection?"

## Remote / Long-Distance

- "How do you maintain connection without being physically together?"
- "What rituals help you feel close across distance?"
- "Time zones, async communication, loneliness?"

## Digital Repair

- "When a text comes out wrong, what is your repair protocol?"
- "Do you have a 'pause' signal for when digital communication escalates?"

## Saturation

Digital boundaries defined + one specific agreement or experiment designed.
